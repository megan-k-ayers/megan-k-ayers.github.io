Hello! I am Megan Ayers, Assistant Professor of Statistics at Reed College. My research focuses on identifying and bridging gaps between causal inference theory and practice, particularly in settings related to environmental policy evaluation and textual data. 

I graduated from Lewis & Clark College with a B.A. in Mathematics and a B.A. in Physics in 2019. I earned my PhD in Statistics & Data Science from Yale University in 2025.
